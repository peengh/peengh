About Peengh

Overpay Karna Mana Hai.

Every phone purchase starts with the same quiet worry: Am I paying too much?

For most of us, a phone is not a small purchase. It is months of savings, a family's contribution, or a bonus we thought twice about spending. And it can still go wrong: you buy at one shop, then a friend finds the same phone Rs 5,000 cheaper the next day or after 2 days you come across a phone with better camera or larger battery in same price.

That feeling is why Peengh exists.

Finding a good phone at a great price used to involve reading reviews on multiple sites, checking online stores one by one and noting down prices, navigating multiple shops in Singapore Plaza, Hafeez Center, Hall Road or Star City Mall etc to find the best deal. Peengh puts that journey in one place, so you can decide with confidence without leaving your seat or bed.

How we make it fair

Trust comes from showing our working, so here it is.

  • We collect. We track listed prices across multiple online stores, updated daily.
  • We compare. Each phone shows its specs and its price at every store, side by side.
  • We rank by price alone. A store can't pay for a better position. If this ever changes, we'll say so on this page & will mention it clearly.
  • We keep PTA honest. PTA-approved and non-PTA phones are shown separately, so you never compare a phone that works on every network with one that still needs tax paid.
  • We don't sell. Peengh doesn't sell phones or handle payments. You buy from the store you choose, on their own website.

What we promise

We don't set prices, and we don't hide them. We show what stores list, and the decision stays with you.

Help us keep it right

Prices change fast, and we won't always catch every change. Before you press "buy", confirm the price, colour, storage and PTA status on the store's page. If it doesn't match what Peengh showed, tell us at contact@peengh.com. We read every report and fix what we find.

P.S

Peengh is built to aid price discovery and if it saves you even one overpaid purchase, it has done its job.
